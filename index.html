Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PRISMWAKE ("the Game") handles information when you play our mobile arcade game.

1. Information We Collect

PRISMWAKE does not require an account to play. The game is designed to store gameplay information such as your best score and game preferences locally on your device.

In the current version, these local values are used to remember your progress and preferences. They are not sent to us by the local storage features themselves.

2. Local Storage

The Game may store your best score and local settings on your device. These values remain on your device unless you remove the app or clear its data. We do not receive these locally stored values through the game's local storage features.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

If the published version contains no analytics, advertising, account services, or third-party SDKs that transmit information, the Game does not send personal information to third parties through those services.

If third-party services are added in a future release, this policy will be updated to describe the information they collect and how it is used or shared.

4. Permissions and Internet Access

The information handled by the Game depends on the permissions and services included in the installed version. We will disclose any material changes to data handling in an updated policy.

5. Data Security and Retention

Locally stored gameplay values remain on your device and are managed by the application. You can remove these values by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the Game.

6. Children's Privacy

PRISMWAKE does not intentionally collect personal information from children through its local gameplay features. If the Game's audience or data practices change, this policy will be reviewed and updated as appropriate.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated revision date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer: prismwake.support@gmail.com
