Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SoloVision ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in PRISMWAKE ("the Game"), an arcade survival game for Android.

This policy applies to the version of PRISMWAKE distributed by SoloVision through Google Play. It describes the information handled by the Game and how users can contact us about privacy.

1. Information We Collect

PRISMWAKE does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to play. The Game is designed to operate without collecting or transmitting players' personal information to SoloVision.

The Game does not include advertisements or analytics in the version covered by this policy. We do not sell users' personal information.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

PRISMWAKE stores your best score locally on your device so that your record can be remembered between sessions. Temporary gameplay information, such as the current score, remaining lives, and gameplay state, is processed on the device to operate the game.

This locally stored gameplay information is not sent to SoloVision by the Game. Local records can generally be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the Game.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

The Game does not sell or share personal information with third parties through its gameplay functionality. The version covered by this policy does not use advertising or analytics services to collect player information.

PRISMWAKE includes a link to this Privacy Policy. When you tap that link, the policy opens in your device's external browser. The browser and website hosting provider may process technical information associated with loading the webpage under their own privacy practices. This browser activity is not the Game transmitting your locally stored score to SoloVision.

4. Permissions and Device Access

The current version of PRISMWAKE does not request runtime permissions to access sensitive device features such as your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or personal photos. The Game stores its best score locally on your device.

The Game does not need an account or access to these sensitive features to provide its core gameplay. Opening this policy uses your device's external browser.

5. Purchases and Payments

If you purchase or download PRISMWAKE through Google Play, payment processing is handled by Google Play under Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. The Game does not collect payment card details directly.

For questions about Google Play billing or transactions, please refer to Google's support resources.

Google Play Help  ·  Google Privacy Policy

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Best scores and other locally retained gameplay data remain on your device until they are cleared or the Game is uninstalled. We do not maintain a PRISMWAKE account database or a server containing these local gameplay records.

7. Children's Privacy

PRISMWAKE does not require personal information to play, and the Game is not designed to collect personal information from children. If the Game's audience, features, or data practices change, we will review this policy and update it as necessary.

8. Security

The Game is designed to keep its gameplay records on the user's device rather than transmitting those records to SoloVision. Users should keep their devices secure and use the device's available security features.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy if the Game's functionality, data practices, or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be published at this same webpage, and the "Last updated" date will be changed accordingly.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer using the details below.
